## Direction contract

THESIS: One continuous journal reading page leads from source-backed facts and
annual metrics to original issues, ordered contents and selected translations.

WORLD: Inherit [DESIGN](../../DESIGN.md): cool white, graphite and restrained
research blue. Preserve factual cover images; unknown information remains text.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact journal name, real cover when saved, readable
introduction and a few common facts. Desktop text owns most of the width. At
390px the page becomes a single column. There is no persistent sidebar or empty
detail pane. Metrics, original issues and compilation contents follow in order.

INTERACTION: Each issue and compilation has a stable URL. Low-frequency facts
and evidence unfold in place. Editing uses a full-width content form, ordered
rows and readable source comparisons; stale saves retain inputs and require
explicit comparison. Existing source/record editors retain their policies.

VERIFICATION: Synthetic public HTTP and real Chromium at1366 and390; no copied
sample values, research files, paid AI or actual data imports. Developer evidence
does not replace the parent's independent review or final release.
